Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Allan Vega / Love Your Dirt ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects the personal information you provide when you use this website (loveyourdirt.com) and its lead-capture form.

When you submit the homebuyer guide request form, we may collect:

  • Full name

Your contact information is retained in our secure Google Sheets database for the purpose of real estate follow-up. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us directly.

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws (including CCPA for California residents) to:
